# 2026.242.2119 — chore(kde-selkies): advance stale pins so published tags carry current refs

## Summary

**3 of this candy's 4 pinned refs** were behind their repos' newest tags. That matters beyond
tidiness: a published tag embeds the pins it carried at tag time, so every consumer pinning a
tag of this candy drags those old refs into its own resolution, and charly reports them as
`resolved to multiple versions` on every build of that consumer.

The candy's own CalVer moves too (`YYYY.DDD.HHMM` at edit time), because changed content must
ship under a new version rather than reusing one already released.

### Why the fix belongs here rather than in the consumer

Advancing a consumer's pin instead does not help and measurably hurts — every option tried in
`distro-cachyos`, on one tree:

| Change in distro-cachyos | Warnings |
|---|---|
| baseline | **2** |
| advance `pod-selkies-core` alone | 6 |
| advance whole selkies family | 8 |
| advance ALL 23 stale pins (of 177) | 8 |

Advancing a consumer to a newer tag exposes that tag's *other* stale refs. The only operation
that removes an edge rather than trading it for others is fixing at the source, so the next
tag cut here carries current pins throughout.

Six such source-level fixes landed today — `layer-chrome-sway#2`, `layer-sway-desktop#3`,
`layer-xfce4-terminal#3`, `layer-sway-desktop-vnc#4`, `pod-charly-automation#4` and
`layer-sway-desktop#4` — each taking its own gate from exit 1 to exit 0 or removing a
measured conflict edge.

Context and the full measurement set: opencharly/distro-cachyos#48.
